# ASA-Force Participant Information

## About this evaluation

You are invited to try ASA-Force, a university workforce-management prototype. The purpose is to evaluate whether the interface and its main workflow are understandable and usable.

ASA-Force is not currently a live employment system. Nothing you do in this evaluation affects employment, attendance, leave, academic results, or university services.

## What you will do

You will be asked to:

- register using fictional details supplied by the researcher;
- wait for the researcher to approve the fictional account;
- sign in and complete a short set of navigation tasks;
- try Arabic and English;
- provide brief feedback about ease of use and problems encountered.

The activity should take approximately 10 to 15 minutes.

## Important data rule

Do not enter any real personal information. In particular, do not provide your real:

- name;
- national identity number;
- telephone number;
- password;
- workplace details;
- location;
- messages, documents, or photographs.

Use only the fictional identity and temporary password supplied by the researcher. Do not reuse a password from any personal account.

## Information recorded

The researcher may record:

- an anonymous participant code;
- whether each task was completed;
- approximate completion time;
- errors or points of confusion;
- voluntary ratings and comments.

The evaluation should not record your real identity or sensitive personal data.

## Voluntary participation

Participation is voluntary. You may stop at any time without providing a reason. Stopping will not affect your studies or relationship with the researcher or university.

If you accidentally enter real information, tell the researcher immediately so it can be removed.

## Agreement

Before beginning, please confirm:

- I understand that ASA-Force is a university prototype.
- I will use only the fictional test information supplied to me.
- I understand what evaluation information will be recorded.
- I understand that participation is voluntary and that I may stop at any time.
- I agree to take part in this usability evaluation.
